About the Business

Brisbane IT & Business Consultancy is a small professional services business based in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. The business provides practical information technology, cybersecurity and business technology services to small and medium-sized organisations.

The business network is designed around an OpenWrt router and firewall, protected internal workstations and a dedicated web server.
